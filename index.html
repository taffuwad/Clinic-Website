04 — PREVENTIVE IMMUNOLOGY

Vaccinations are
essential to protect
your health.

Proactive immunization forms the cornerstone of individual and communal resilience. At Maison, we administer refined, preservative-free formulations in a calming, monitored clinical setting with antibody titre verification.

INFLUENZA +
Annual quadrivalent formulated vaccine safeguarding against active influenza A and B strains. Recommended every autumn for optimal viral defense.
PNEUMONIA +
Conjugate pneumococcal immunization (PCV20) defending against invasive Streptococcus pneumoniae respiratory infections and systemic bacteremia.
SHINGLES +
Recombinant zoster 2-dose series offering over 90% efficacy against herpes zoster reactivation and debilitating post-herpetic neuralgia.
HPV +
9-valent human papillomavirus coverage protecting against high-risk oncogenic strains associated with cervical and oropharyngeal malignancies.
HEPATITIS A +
Inactivated viral series providing robust, lifelong hepatic protection against contaminated food and water-borne pathogens for travelers and executives.
HEPATITIS B +
Recombinant surface antigen immunization guarding against chronic viral hepatitis, cirrhosis, and hepatocellular carcinoma.
